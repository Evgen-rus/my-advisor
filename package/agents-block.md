## My Advisor

Use the project skill `$my-advisor` for a direct request, before a risky decision or an ambiguous change to databases, integrations, queues, schedulers, or concurrency, after two evidence-based attempts fail, or before finishing complex work with a concrete unverified risk. Follow the skill's instructions to consult the separate Astra agent. The primary agent owns implementation and verification.
